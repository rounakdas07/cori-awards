Method and limits

Purpose and product boundary. This is a sales-research worklist for Cori’s own team. Its 7 October funding announcement names commercial expansion and reports academic customers alongside CROs. Cori’s product description already covers trial precedents, design, budgets and regulatory review; this brief addresses finding and qualifying prospective users. Existing customer relationships and Cori’s private pipeline are unknown.

Sources and dates. The NIHR Research and Training Awards dataset was retrieved on 25 November 2026. Its metadata says the edition covers data to 31 March 2026, although it was modified on 29 October and contains later contracted project starts. It is a lagged funding snapshot, not a complete list of awards announced this year. The script also queried the public ClinicalTrials.gov API and ISRCTN registry by award identifier on the collection date.

Filters and cleaning. Retrieve all awards with a contract start on or after 25 November 2025, then retain starts through 25 November 2026; research awards marked active or contracted; a valid UK host postcode; an explicit oncology term in the title; and an explicit trial or first-in-human title, or a stated prospective clinical-trial plan in the abstract. Exclude follow-up-only awards, evidence syntheses, consortia, training, and records without the required evidence. Deduplicate by NIHR award ID, normalise whitespace and retain missing investigator names as missing. 348 rows became 7; the quality report records all exclusions and the complete retained sample.

Scoring. One point each for a published investigator, a contract start on or after 26 May 2026, and explicit multi-centre, multi-site or umbrella wording about the funded trial. Statements about a hypothetical follow-on study do not earn the design point. Highest total first; ties favour a named lead, then a later contract start, then a larger original award. Recommend the first three distinct organisations with named leads. This is a transparent order for research calls, not a measure of clinical quality, purchasing likelihood or operational difficulty.

Registry linkage. The exact NIHR award IDs were queried together, plus any NCT or ISRCTN IDs stated in the awards. Neither registry returned a record for that query. This check does not search every title variation, CTIS or local registers, and missing grant references are possible. All seven recruitment stages therefore remain unknown here; none is labelled “not yet recruiting”.

Coverage and remaining questions. The strict title rule misses oncology projects whose titles do not name the disease. A UK host does not establish every trial site’s country. The two Birmingham umbrella awards lack investigator names in this export. Named researchers are not verified purchasing contacts, no email is guessed, and public funding does not establish unspent funds, unmet demand or an open procurement. The collection and build scripts retain the exact rules, source hashes and retrieval timestamps. NIHR data is reused under the Open Government Licence v3.0.
